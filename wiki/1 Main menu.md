# Main menu

![Main menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Davesc0/LC-ClickableTerminal/main/docs/MainMenu.png)

The game's own commands, one row each:

- **MOONS**: the moons the autopilot can route to.
- **STORE**: items, vehicles, ship upgrades, decorations, suits, and your saved carts.
- **BESTIARY**: the creatures you have scanned.
- **STORAGE**: what is put away in storage.
- **OTHER**: monitor, transmit, scan, logs, eject.
- **EXTRAS**: ship buttons added by this mod (where they go is the Extras setting).
- **MODS**: everything the installed mods add.

## Status
On the right is how the run stands: day, quota, scrap on the ship, deadline, the ship, the moon and its
weather, and the deaths. On a moon the time is shown under the buttons at the top.

## Windows
Info pages, questions and short answers come up in a window over the page. Close it with its X or BACK.
Anything that costs money asks first in a window, so a stray click doesn't spend your credits.

## Text size
Every page has its own text size. The - and + buttons at the top change it for that page until you close
the game. TextScale is for the pages that don't have a size of their own.

## Controller
No cursor, the chosen thing gets a frame. The d-pad or the left stick steps between things, LB and RB switch
tabs, the triggers turn a page, A clicks, X is the right click, B goes back.

## Settings
- **OwnPages**: off gives you the game's own text pages, the mouse and controller still work on them.
- **RememberLastPage**: the terminal opens on the page you left it on.
- **SkipWelcome**: skip the welcome screen.
- **ScreenAlwaysOn**: the screen stays on with the last page when nobody uses it (only on your side).
- **DebugLog**: writes what the terminal loads and shows to the log. Turn it on when you report a bug.
- **SharpTerminal**, **ZoomToScreen**, **CursorSpeed**, **LineScroll**, **ScrollLines**: how the terminal looks and moves.
