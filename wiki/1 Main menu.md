# Main menu

![Main menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Davesc0/LC-ClickableTerminal/main/docs/main-menu.png)

The game's own commands, one row each:

- **MOONS**: the moons the autopilot can route to.
- **STORE**: items, vehicles, ship upgrades, decorations, suits.
- **BESTIARY**: the creatures you have scanned.
- **STORAGE**: what is put away in storage.
- **OTHER**: monitor, switch, ping, flash, transmit, scan, logs, eject.
- **EXTRAS**: buttons of the ship, from here (can be turned off).
- **MODS**: everything the installed mods add.

The terminal opens on the page you left it on (RememberLastPage). The welcome screen can be skipped
(SkipWelcome).
