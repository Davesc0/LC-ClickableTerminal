# Storage, Scan, Other, Extras

## Storage
What is put away in storage. Click a row to take it out.

## Scan
The scrap left on the moon, with the total on top.

## Other
- **VIEW MONITOR**: a switch, turns the ship's map on the terminal on or off.
- **SWITCH**, **PING**, **FLASH**: a list of the players and radar boosters to pick from.
- **TRANSMIT**: type the message, then SEND or CANCEL. Without a signal translator it tells you so.
- **SCAN**, **LOGS** (Sigurd's logs), **EJECT** (with a confirm page).

## Extras
Buttons of the ship, from the terminal. Turn the page off with ShowExtras.
- **SHIP DOORS**: open or close, only while landed.
- **TELEPORTER** / **INVERSE TELEPORTER**: when you have them, shows when they are charging.
- **LIGHTS**: on or off.
- **LEVER**: land or take off, asks first.
- **RANDOM MOON**: picks a moon you can fly to, you still confirm.
