# Mods page

Everything the installed mods add to the terminal.

On top are the mods this terminal has pages for, with a line about what they do. Some get a folder
of their own:

- **Lategame Upgrades**: upgrade store, contracts, interns, extend deadline, weather probe and the rest.
- **Sell My Scrap**: sell quota, sell all, sell an amount (set with buttons), sell a kind of scrap
  from a list, the scrap on the ship, every scrap in the game.
- **Scrap Bank**: balance, store all, store an amount, store single items from a list, sell from the bank.
- **Interiors** (Selectable Interiors): pick the interior of the next moon.
- **Seed Picker** (Terminal Seed Picker): see, randomize or type the next moon's seed.

Single commands: Ship Inventory, MergeScrap, Revive, Weather Probe, Buy Extra Day, Too Much Scrap.

Every **Interactive Terminal API** app works with the mouse and the controller. BACK steps back
inside it and leaves it, MENU closes it.

The last folder, **UNLISTED**, holds the commands of mods this terminal doesn't know, by name only.

Your own entries: ExtraCommands in the config ("Label=what to type", separated by semicolons).
