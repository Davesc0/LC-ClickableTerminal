# Bestiary

![Bestiary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Davesc0/LC-ClickableTerminal/main/docs/Bestiary-main.png)

The creatures you have scanned. The panel beside the list shows the one the cursor is on: its picture,
a sentence or two from its file, and its figures: danger, health, power, how many can be out at once,
whether it can be stunned, and where it lives. Click it to open the whole file in a window, with its
picture or video (NO IMAGE if there is none). Opening it counts as reading it, like the game's own page.

![Creature file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Davesc0/LC-ClickableTerminal/main/docs/Bestiary-detailed.png)

- **ShowAllCreatures**: list every creature file without scanning it first. Nothing is saved, the
  files close again when you turn it off.
- **GroupCreaturesByAuthor**: one section per creature maker, the game's own first, OTHER last.
