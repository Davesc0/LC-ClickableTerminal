# Bestiary

The creatures you have scanned. A creature's page shows its figures on top: health, damage, power,
how many can be out at once, whether it can be stunned, and where it lives. Under them the game's
own file text.

- **ShowAllCreatures**: list every creature file without scanning it first. Nothing is saved, the
  files close again when you turn it off.
- **GroupCreaturesByAuthor**: one section per creature maker, the game's own first, OTHER last.
